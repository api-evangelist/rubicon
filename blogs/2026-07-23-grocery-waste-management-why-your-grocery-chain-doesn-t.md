Managing grocery waste today involves far more than trash collection. Leading grocery chains must coordinate recycling, food waste management, organics diversion, vendor performance, sustainability reporting, and regulatory compliance across hundreds of locations. The challenge isn’t simply managing waste – it’s managing environmental operations.
