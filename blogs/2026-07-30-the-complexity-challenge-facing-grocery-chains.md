As grocery chains grow, so does the complexity of managing waste, recycling, food recovery, vendors, equipment, and sustainability initiatives. Without visibility across every location, small operational issues can quickly become enterprise-wide challenges. A grocery chain with 500 stores doesn’t have one environmental operations program.
