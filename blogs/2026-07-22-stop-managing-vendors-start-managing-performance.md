Most waste, recycling, and sustainability programs weren’t designed to work together, they grew over time. As organizations expanded locations and added new services, they also added vendors, reporting tools, recycling programs, and operational processes. Each solved an immediate need, but together they often created a much bigger challenge: disconnected operations.
